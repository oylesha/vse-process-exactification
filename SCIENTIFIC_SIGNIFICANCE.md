## Why this release matters

This repository is not presented as a completed Theory of Everything. Its central contribution is a process-oriented exactification architecture for generative mathematical/physical systems in which a structural state is not conflated with the event or history that produced it.

The core runtime is

$$
FULL \to SATURATE \to JOINT \to TRANSFORM \to RECOVER\_TEST
\to SAFE\_QUOTIENT \to FACTOR \to RESIDUAL \to REIFY/CLASSIFY.
$$

The three operational rules are:

$$
SATURATE\ before\ TRANSFORM,
$$

$$
JOINT\ before\ MARGINAL,
$$

$$
RECOVER\ before\ QUOTIENT.
$$

### 3. Joint relation data can retain information lost by marginals

For cyclic phase carriers, off-diagonal labeled Gram entries can recover phase that is invisible to diagonal magnitude or unlabeled spectrum.

This motivates the use of a full labeled joint relation tensor before destructive quotienting.
