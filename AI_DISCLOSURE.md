# AI Assistance Disclosure

This project used AI systems extensively as research assistants.

AI-assisted activities include:
- literature discovery and comparison;
- symbolic and numerical computation;
- code drafting, testing, and debugging;
- counterexample search and adversarial audit;
- organization of long research histories;
- drafting and editing of exposition.

The human author supplied the research direction, hypotheses, problem selection, conceptual decisions, and final responsibility for the claims. AI tools are not listed as authors.
