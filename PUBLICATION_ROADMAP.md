## Language policy

The publication-facing layer of this project uses **English as the primary language** for international discoverability, peer review, indexing, and citation.

Original research notes may remain in Russian inside the private archive. Translation is treated as an editorial layer and must not rewrite the historical record.

## Publication sequence

### Stage P0 — Public priority record (NOW)

Purpose:
- establish authorship and a public date;
- freeze the bounded technical core;
- expose reproducible evidence;
- avoid grand claims that are not yet proven.

Publish:
1. authorship / ORCID / citation metadata;
2. process exactification architecture;
3. STATE / EVENT / LINEAGE / MACRO / WORLD separation;
4. source-preserving D2 audit;
5. joint-relation / safe-quotient result;
6. project-specific q5/q7 cross-block → M5(C);
7. scoped recurrent internal pole certificates;
8. explicit open/conditional claim ledger.

Do not present as solved:
- Riemann hypothesis;
- P vs NP;
- completed physical Theory of Everything;
- physical Monster symmetry;
- numerical first-principles prediction of constants.
